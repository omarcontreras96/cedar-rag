## Q1: are both required passages selected?

- **Keyword:** yes, both A1 and A2. But A1's score of 3 includes the token `00`,
  which `19:00` shares with `08:00`/`22:00`. `student` does not match `students`.
  Part of the correct result comes from a tokenization accident.
- **Embedding:** no. It selected A2 and T1, so the evidence is partial (1/2). A1 ranks
  4th at 0.374249, below T1 (0.581953) and B1 (0.532696). A2 alone shows the
  operation would need two trained people. The time condition (08:00–22:00 access
  hours) is in A1, which was not retrieved.

## Q2 and Q3: do the paraphrases retrieve the intended passage?

- **Q2:** both methods rank A2 first. The keyword hit depends on the shared words
  `laser` and `present`. "Without another person" and "two trained people" share
  no words, so the paraphrase itself didn't match. Embedding: A2 0.585193, which
  leads the next passage, T1 0.434761, by about 0.15.
- **Q3, a useful embedding match:** T1 scores 0.742027, the highest score in all four
  runs and well ahead of B1 at 0.471195. "Frequently / refresh / qualification" share
  no words with "renewed every 12 months / training", but the embedding still
  connects them.
- **Q3, a fragile keyword match:** T1 scored 2 on `laser` and `must` only. None of
  the paraphrase's content words matched. The original hit relied on the word "must".

## Q4: why do passages come back when there is no answer?

- The embedding retriever always returns the top k by design (no threshold).
  Every pair of vectors has some cosine similarity. Here, T1 at 0.347721 and A2 at
  0.320827 are close mainly because they mention a laser, not because either states
  a wavelength. A positive score measures topical closeness, not answerability.
- In this run a cutoff near 0.36 would have rejected both Q4 passages (0.347721, 0.320827) while keeping every required passage (lowest: A1 at 0.374249 for Q1). The margin is only about 0.027, and the same cutoff would still pass irrelevant passages such as B1 for Q1 (0.532696). Four questions cannot justify a threshold.
- Keyword: A2 and B1 score 1 each on `laser` alone. `wavelength` matched nothing,
  which the score printout shows, but the program still returns them because their
  scores are positive.
- Neither method's output can be counted as answer retrieval for Q4. Detecting the
  missing information needs a separate check.

- The ranking does not change with k. Only the cut-off moves. A1 is 4th, so in
  this run it would first be included at k = 4.
- The k = 3 extra passages: T1 adds a relevant condition (a current training record
  is required before operating the laser). B1 (booking limited to two hours per
  session) is irrelevant to whether someone may operate the laser alone at 19:00.
  Raising k added one useful condition and one irrelevant passage, but not the
  missing time evidence.
